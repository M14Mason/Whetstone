Re-reading is not studying

Most study apps give you a pile of cards and let you shuffle them. You end up drilling the easy ones because they feel good, and quietly avoiding the three topics that will actually cost you the grade.

What makes this different

◎

It knows your actual timetable

Add AP Biology, Honors Algebra 2 and English 11 and you get those courses, broken into the units your teacher is actually teaching. Other tools make you search a library and hope someone made a decent set.

↻

It hunts your weak spots

Every answer moves a rating for that specific topic. Get Mendelian genetics wrong twice and it comes back tomorrow, then next week, then only once it has stuck. You do not choose what to revise; the gaps choose.

✎

Real AP exam practice

Section timings, question counts and weightings taken from the official exam pages, plus free-response prompts with the real scoring rubrics. Where an exam has not been verified yet, it says so rather than inventing a format.

⚡

Five ways to study one set

Adaptive practice, flashcards, a timed match game, full practice tests and a queue of only the questions you have missed. Same content, different pressure.
